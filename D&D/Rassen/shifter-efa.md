# Shifter
*Source: Eberron: Forge of the Artificer p. 37*  
![](3-Mechanics/CLI/races/img/shifter.webp#right)

- **Ability Scores**: None
- **Type**: humanoid
- **Size**: Small or Medium
- **Speed**: 30 ft.

## Traits

### Bestial Instincts

Channeling the beast within, you gain proficiency in one of the following skills of your choice: Acrobatics, Athletics, Intimidation, or Survival.

### Darkvision

You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

### Shifting

As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, you can shape-shift to assume a more bestial appearance. This transformation lasts for 1 minute or until you revert to your normal appearance as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>. When you shift, you gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> equal to 2 times your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>. You can shift a number of times equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.

Whenever you shift, you gain the benefit of one of the following options (choose when you select this species)

- **Beasthide.** You gain `1d6` additional <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span>. While shifted, you have a +1 bonus to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Armor Class</span>.  
- **Longtooth.** When you shift and as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span> on your other turns while shifted, you can use your elongated fangs to make an <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Unarmed Strike</span>. If you hit with this <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Unarmed Strike</span> and deal damage, you can deal Piercing damage equal to `1d6` plus your Strength modifier, instead of the normal damage of an <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Unarmed Strike</span>.  
- **Swiftstride.** While you are shifted, your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Speed</span> increases by 10 feet. Additionally, you can move up to 10 feet as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> when a creature ends its turn within 5 feet of you. This reactive movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks.  
- **Wildhunt.** While shifted, you have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on Wisdom checks. Additionally, no creature within 30 feet of you can have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on an attack roll against you unless you have the Incapacitated condition.  

## Description

Shifters—sometimes called "weretouched"—descend from people who contracted full or partial lycanthropy. Humanoids with a bestial aspect, shifters can't change shape fully, but they can enhance their animalistic features temporarily in a process they call "shifting."

Shifters resemble humans in height and build but are typically more lithe and flexible. Their facial features have a bestial cast, often with large eyes and pointed ears; most shifters also possess prominent canine teeth. They grow fur-like hair on nearly every part of their bodies. While a shifter's appearance might remind an onlooker of an animal, the shifter remains clearly identifiable as a Humanoid even when at their most feral.
