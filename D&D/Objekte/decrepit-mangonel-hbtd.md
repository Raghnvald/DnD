# Decrepit Mangonel
*Source: Hold Back The Dead p. 5*  

Loading a Decrepit Mangonel requires two Utilize actions, and aiming it requires one Utilize action. Then a creature can take the Sack of Hands action.

## Actions

***Sack of Hands (Requires Load and Aim).*** A launched sack of writhing hands lands in a space 30 to 100 feet away from the mangonel. A Swarm of Crawling Claws appears where the sack lands.
