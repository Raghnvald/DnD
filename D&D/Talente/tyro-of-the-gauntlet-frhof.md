# Tyro of the Gauntlet
*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 38*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Stand as One.** When an ally within 5 feet of you is subjected to an effect that would push or pull it, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to prevent that ally from being pushed or pulled. To receive this benefit, the ally can't have the Incapacitated condition.

**Vigilant.** When you take the Ready action, the next attack roll made against you has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> before the start of your next turn.
