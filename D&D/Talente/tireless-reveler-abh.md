# Tireless Reveler
*Source: Astarion's Book of Hungers p. 8*  

When an ally you can see within 60 feet of yourself expends <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Heroic Inspiration</span>, you can gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Heroic Inspiration</span> if you lack it. You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Short</span> or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.
