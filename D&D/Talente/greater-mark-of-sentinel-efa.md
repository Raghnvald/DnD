# Greater Mark of Sentinel
*Source: Eberron: Forge of the Artificer p. 44*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Improved Intuition.** When you use the Sentinel's Intuition benefit of your [Mark of Sentinel](3-Mechanics/CLI/feats/mark-of-sentinel-efa.md) feat, you can roll `1d6` instead of `1d4`.

**Improved Sentinel.** When you use the Vigilant Guardian benefit of your [Mark of Sentinel](3-Mechanics/CLI/feats/mark-of-sentinel-efa.md) feat, you can also make one attack with a weapon or an <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Unarmed Strike</span> as part of that same <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span>.
