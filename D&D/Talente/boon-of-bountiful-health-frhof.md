# Boon of Bountiful Health
*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 42*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Augmented Health.** When you gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span>, increase the number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> you gain by 5.

**Superior Recuperation.** When you spend one or more <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Point Dice</span> to regain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>, you can instead use the highest number possible for each die.
