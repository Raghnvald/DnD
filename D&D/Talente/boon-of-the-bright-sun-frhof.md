# Boon of the Bright Sun
*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 43*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Constitution, Wisdom, or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Daylight Presence.** As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, you radiate a 30-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bright Light</span> that is sunlight. If any of the <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span>'s area overlaps with an area of Darkness created by a spell, that spell is dispelled. The <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> lasts until you dismiss it (no action required), die, or have the Incapacitated condition.

**Fortifying Light.** When your Daylight Presence is active, at the start of each of your turns, you and allies you can see in your Daylight Presence gain 10 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span>.
