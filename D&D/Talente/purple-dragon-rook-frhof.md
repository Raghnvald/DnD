# Purple Dragon Rook
*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 38*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Entreat.** You gain proficiency in one of the following skills: Insight, Performance, or Persuasion.

**Rallying Cry.** When you roll <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> and don't have the Incapacitated condition, you can choose a number of creatures equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span> that you can see within 30 feet of yourself. Those creatures gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Heroic Inspiration</span>.

Once you use this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.
