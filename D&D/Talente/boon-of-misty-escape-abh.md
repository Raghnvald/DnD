# Boon of Misty Escape
*Source: Astarion's Book of Hungers p. 11*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Gaseous Form.** If you drop to 0 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> but aren't killed outright, you can instead drop to 1 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Point</span> and cast Gaseous Form without expending a spell slot (no action required). When you cast this spell this way, you can target only yourself, your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Fly Speed</span> is 20 feet, and you regain 10 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> at the start of each of your turns for the spell's duration. The spell's spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.

Once you use this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.
