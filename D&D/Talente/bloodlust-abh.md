# Bloodlust
*Source: Astarion's Book of Hungers p. 9*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Powerful Recovery.** When you roll a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Point Die</span> to regain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>, you can treat any roll of 1 or 2 as a 3.

**Sanguine Feast.** Once per turn when you hit a Bloodied creature that isn't a Construct or Undead with an attack roll, you can expend a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Point Die</span>, roll it, and regain a number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> equal to the number rolled plus your Constitution modifier.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.
