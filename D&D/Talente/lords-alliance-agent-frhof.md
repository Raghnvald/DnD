# Lords' Alliance Agent
*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 38*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Inspiring Strike.** Once per turn when you score a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Critical Hit</span> against a creature, you can choose an ally within 30 feet of yourself who can see or hear you and who lacks <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Heroic Inspiration</span>. That ally gains <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Heroic Inspiration</span>.

**Reassert Honor.** When an enemy you can see deals damage to an ally of yours that is within 5 feet of you, you have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on your next attack roll against that enemy before the end of your next turn.
