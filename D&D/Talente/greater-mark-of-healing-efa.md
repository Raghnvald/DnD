# Greater Mark of Healing
*Source: Eberron: Forge of the Artificer p. 44*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Improved Intuition.** When you use the Medical Intuition benefit of your [Mark of Healing](3-Mechanics/CLI/feats/mark-of-healing-efa.md) feat, you can roll `1d6` instead of `1d4`.

**Improved Healing.** You can now use the Healing Touch benefit of your [Mark of Healing](3-Mechanics/CLI/feats/mark-of-healing-efa.md) feat to cast Cure Wounds without using a spell slot a number of times equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>. Additionally, when you cast Cure Wounds and roll dice to determine the number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> restored, you can treat any 1 or 2 on a roll as a 3.
