# Boon of Looming Shadows
*Source: Astarion's Book of Hungers p. 11*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Shadowy Stretch.** When you take the Attack action, your reach for your Melee weapon attacks increases by 10 feet until the end of your turn.

**Dancing Silhouette.** You can take the Dodge action as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>.
