# Spellfire Spark
*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 38*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Magic Absorption.** Once per turn, when you take damage from a spell or magical effect, you reduce the total damage taken by `1d4`. You can't use this benefit if you have the Incapacitated condition.

**Spellfire Flame.** You learn the Sacred Flame cantrip. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell (choose when you select this feat). You can also cast this cantrip as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span> a number of times equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.
