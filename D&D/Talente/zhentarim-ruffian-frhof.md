# Zhentarim Ruffian
*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 38*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Exploit Opening.** When you roll damage for an Opportunity Attack, you can roll the damage dice twice and use either roll against the target.

**Family First.** If you have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Heroic Inspiration</span> when you roll <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span>, you can expend it to give yourself and your allies <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on that <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Initiative</span> roll.
