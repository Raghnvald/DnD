# Greater Mark of Warding
*Source: Eberron: Forge of the Artificer p. 45*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Improved Intuition.** When you use the Warder's Intuition benefit of your [Mark of Warding](3-Mechanics/CLI/feats/mark-of-warding-efa.md) feat, you can roll `1d6` instead of `1d4`.

**Improved Warding.** When a creature makes an attack roll against you or a creature you can see within 30 feet of yourself, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to impose <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on that roll. You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.
