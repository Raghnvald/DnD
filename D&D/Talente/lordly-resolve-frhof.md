# Lordly Resolve
*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 40*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Standard Bearer.** As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, choose up to three creatures within 60 feet of yourself that can see you. Each target can immediately take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to right itself and end the Prone condition, provided its <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Speed</span> isn't 0. Additionally, you bolster the targets' resolve, which lasts for 1 minute or until you have the Incapacitated condition. While bolstered, a target can't be possessed or gain the Charmed or Frightened condition; if a target is already possessed, Charmed, or Frightened, the target has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on any new saving throw against the relevant effect.

Once you use this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.
