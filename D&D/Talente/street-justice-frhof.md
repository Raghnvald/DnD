# Street Justice
*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 41*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Headlock.** Your allies have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on attack rolls against a creature Grappled by you.

**Sturdy Knot.** When you use Chain, Manacles, or Rope to bind a creature, add your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span> to the DC to escape or burst the Chain, Manacles, or Rope.

**Tough Talk.** A creature's <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hostile</span> attitude doesn't impose <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on your Charisma (Intimidation) checks to influence that creature.
