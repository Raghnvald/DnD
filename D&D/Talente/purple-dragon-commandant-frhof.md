# Purple Dragon Commandant
*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 40*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th or Proficiency with martial weapons

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Encourage Ally.** As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>, you bolster one ally you can see within 30 feet. The ally gains <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> equal to `2d6` plus the modifier of the ability score increased by this feat. You can take this <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span> a number of times equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>, and you regain all uses when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.

**Last Stand.** You have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on attack rolls while Bloodied.
