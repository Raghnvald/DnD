# Boon of Bloodshed
*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 42*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Killer's Fortune.** When an enemy you can see is reduced to 0 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span>, you gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on the next attack roll you make before the end of your next turn.

**Power from Pain.** Once per turn, when you make an attack roll while Bloodied, you can deal extra damage to the target equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>. The extra damage's type is the same as the attack's type.
