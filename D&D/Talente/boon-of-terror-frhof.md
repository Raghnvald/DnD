# Boon of Terror
*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 43*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Fearless.** You have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to the Frightened condition.

**Flee, Fools!.** When a creature with the Frightened condition starts its turn within 60 feet of you, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to stoke its terror, provided you can see the creature and it isn't behind <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Total Cover</span>. If you do so, the creature must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC 8 plus your Charisma modifier and your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span>) or spend its turn moving away from you by the fastest available means. Once you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Short</span> or <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.

**Intimidating.** You gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency</span> in the Intimidation skill if you don't already have it. You also gain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Expertise</span> in Intimidation.
