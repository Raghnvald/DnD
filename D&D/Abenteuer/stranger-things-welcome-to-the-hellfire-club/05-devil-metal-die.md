# Devil, Metal, Die!
*Source: Stranger Things: Welcome to the Hellfire Club* 

*An Adventure for Level 3 Characters*

![](3-Mechanics/CLI/adventures/stranger-things-welcome-to-the-hellfire-club/img/063-04-06-devil-metal-die.webp#center)

This adventure is designed to fill one or two sessions of play.

If you aren't using the Map of Greyhawkins, it can begin in any tavern before descending into the Nine Hells.

## Key Plot Points

This information is key to the adventure's story:

![](3-Mechanics/CLI/adventures/stranger-things-welcome-to-the-hellfire-club/img/064-dardew-sticker.webp#center)

- **Plot Point 1.** Dardew invites the characters to compete in the Hellfire Games. If they accept, they're transported to the Hellfire Dome.  
- **Plot Point 2.** In the Hellfire Dome, the characters face off against devils, demons, and a dragon unlike any other.  
- **Plot Point 3.** Spectators shower the characters with gold throughout the competition. If a character falls in battle, Dardew intercedes and offers them the chance to rise and fight again as part of an infernal pact.  
- **Plot Point 4.** The characters either complete all the challenges or die trying. Any survivors are inducted into the Hellfire Club.

## Adventure Background

From the fires of the Nine Hells rises the infamous Hellfire Dome, a diabolical arena where contestants of ill repute vie for gold and glory. Death, betrayal, and priceless treasures await those who endure the lethal games in the dome, which are spectated by an audience of disorderly fiends.

![](3-Mechanics/CLI/adventures/stranger-things-welcome-to-the-hellfire-club/img/067-demodragon-sticker.webp#center)

Contestants must rely on strength and wits to overcome the diabolical gauntlet that awaits them in the dome. The last and greatest of these trials is a showdown with the [Demodragon](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/demodragon-wtthc.md): a wretched, two-headed terror created by Demogorgon, the Prince of Demons. Those who slay the Demodragon depart the Nine Hells as wealthy champions.

Winners are also inducted into the Hellfire Club, an exclusive association of infernal origin respected by devils of every rank. Members of the Hellfire Club have a reputation for being tough, fearless, and above all—totally metal.

Every six years, the Hellfire Dome opens its doors to non-Fiends, allowing them to compete in a series of infernal challenges. Dardew the Banished, a cambion with aspirations of grandeur, invites the characters to compete in the arena. If victorious, the characters will be crowned members of the legendary Hellfire Club, and [Dardew](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/dardew-wtthc.md) will rise in the fiery ranks. However, if the characters lose, they might forfeit their souls.

### Dardew the Banished

This year's master of ceremonies for the Hellfire Games is a music-loving fiend named Dardew the Banished. A lowly cambion, Dardew hopes to ascend the ranks of the Nine Hells through panache and showmanship, impressing powerful devils in the audience with an unforgettable performance. He believes the characters are his ticket to fame.

## Deal with the Devil

Few survive the Hellfire Dome in one piece, but those who make deals with devils have a better chance at making it out alive. Early deaths among contestants can make for an underwhelming display, and Dardew can't afford for this year's Hellfire Games to disappoint.

To give the characters an edge in the arena, Dardew has arranged a special contract to keep them fighting beyond the brink of death. Still, no fiend can resist corrupting a mortal soul, especially one that belongs to a good-hearted adventurer. The characters would be wise to consider the contract's fine print before they sign.

> [!note] 
> 
> **Souls for sale**! Death is a part of the game, but it doesn't have to be the end. And in this adventure, it can be fun for everyone involved! Celebrate players who push their luck and amplify this great, bloody, gladiatorial spectacle. You can always give them their characters' souls back later... **if you're a pushover**.
^0d7

### Infernal Contract

Whenever a character drops to 0 Hit Points in the Hellfire Dome, Dardew extends an infernal contract to that character. This event takes place outside the Initiative order, which resumes after the character accepts or declines Dardew's offer.

Read or paraphrase the following text:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> The scene around you freezes as Dardew appears and unfurls a lengthy contract. "I've got a good feeling about you. How would you like to keep going?"
^0d9

Show that player the Infernal Contract Handout and ask them if their character agrees to the following terms:

- **Defying Death.** When you drop to 0 Hit Points while in the Hellfire Dome—including when you are first offered this contract—you can roll any number of d6s (minimum of `3d6`) and regain a number of Hit Points equal to the roll's total (no action required). If you roll a 6 on three or more of these dice, you also forfeit your soul and can't benefit from the contract in this way again.  
- **Forfeiting Your Soul.** If you forfeit your soul, it becomes the property of Dardew the Banished on your death.  
- **Voiding the Contract.** As a [Magic](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/actions.md#Magic) action, Dardew or an archdevil can void this contract, returning any forfeited souls to their previous owners. Destroying the contract or its arbiter doesn't void the agreement.  

If the character accepts these terms, they sign the contract. Dardew then collects the contract and vanishes in a cloud of brimstone. Dardew reappears to offer the same contract to any character who drops to 0 Hit Points while in the Hellfire Dome, even if the character previously rejected the contract.

> [!note] This is Hellfire.
> 
> If the characters reject Dardew, your players are either scaredy cats or a bunch of *****. Either way, put on your favorite mix and wait 'em out. They'll come to their senses eventually.
> 
> It's D&D or bust.
^this-is-hellfire

## Beginning the Adventure

When the players are ready to begin the adventure, read or paraphrase the following text:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> You sit in an unremarkable tavern somewhere in Greyhawkins when, in a burst of brimstone, a winged, red-skinned fiend with a mane of curly hair and a boyish smile appears. He lands atop one of the tables with an exaggerated bow.
> 
> "Greetings, mortal adventurers. My name is Dardew. I hope I'm not interrupting your meal," he says coyly.
> 
> "I've come to invite you to compete in the Hellfire Games. I've been watching you for some time now, and I believe you just might have what it takes to join the esteemed Hellfire Club. Care to play the devils' game?"
^0dc

Dardew has come to recruit the characters for the Hellfire Games. He is [Indifferent](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/indifferent-attitude-xphb.md) toward the party—but still charming—and shares the following information about the competition:

- **Hellfire Games.** The Hellfire Games are a sexennial competition in which contestants battle for prestige, treasure, and induction into the exclusive Hellfire Club.  
- **Hellfire Club.** The Hellfire Club is an invite-only club whose members include epic heroes, high-ranking devils, and figures of legend.  

Hellfire Dome. The games are held in the Hellfire Dome, an arena far from Greyhawkins; Dardew is this year's host and can take the characters there.

If the characters ask about the risk, Dardew explains that while the Hellfire Games are dangerous, the characters have nothing to worry about. He has prepared a special contract to help the characters cheat death (see "Deal with the Devil"), though he doesn't delve into specifics.

### Accepting the Invitation

If the characters accept Dardew's invitation, he magically transports them to the Hellfire Dome. Read or paraphrase the following text:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> "That's the spirit!" Dardew laughs and claps his hands, and a cloud of red smoke engulfs you.
^0de

The characters begin the games in area H1 of the Hellfire Dome.

### Declining the Invitation

If the characters decline, read or paraphrase:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> "Hmm, not what I expected. Oh, well! Maybe next time." Dardew shrugs and disappears with a puff of smoke.
^0e0

At your discretion, Dardew might return later to reissue his invitation, perhaps offering an important insight or piece of information in return for the characters' participation.

## Hellfire Dome

Devils prize competition, treachery, and power, so they created the Hellfire Dome, a gauntlet of deadly scenarios designed to feature all three. These challenges take place in a volcanic pit on Avernus, the first layer of the Nine Hells. Just outside the arid dome lies an endless wasteland razed in the Blood War, an eternal conflict between devils and demons.

The Hellfire Dome is a multistory arena hewn from obsidian. A massive dome forged from infernal steel covers the arena, shielding it from meteors that fall from Avernus's skies. Beneath the dome, an infernal steel cage prevents contestants from escaping.

Thousands of devils gather to watch the Hellfire Games. These rowdy onlookers sometimes cast coins into the pit, showering contenders with gold. Unpopular contestants occasionally receive a ball of flame hurled by a disgruntled fiend.

Refer to the Hellfire Dome map and note that the players begin in the center of area H1. Don't reveal the rest of the map's contents yet, as it is not not initially visible to the characters.

### Hellfire Dome Features

The Hellfire Dome has the following features:

- **Ambiance.** The arena is dry and oppressively hot, and it reeks of blood and brimstone. The audience roars with cheers and howls.  
- **Ceilings.** Aside from the dome over the arena, the Hellfire Dome has no ceilings. An indestructible cage 60 feet above the pit separates contestants from the audience.  
- **Lighting.** Unless otherwise specified, areas in the dome are in [Bright Light](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/bright-light-xphb.md).  
- **Portals.** Each of the lettered portals on Hellfire Dome map is a gateway of harmless fire linked to another area in the arena. A creature that enters a portal emerges within 5 feet of the portal with the matching letter on Hellfire Dome map or in the nearest unoccupied space.

The five [Demobats](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/demobat-wtthc.md) fight to the death. Whenever the characters vanquish a Demobat, the audience rewards them (see "Treasure" below). Once the characters defeat the bats, five blazing portals appear along the arena's walls.

- **Portal E**. Portal E leads to area H2.  

Each portal initially functions as a one-way door, preventing reentry until the characters have completed the challenge in the destination area.

#### H2: Fiend Feud

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> Dardew hovers impatiently over this room, which features two rusty, wrought-iron podiums that resemble corroded coffins. Five masked cultists stand behind one of the podiums, while the other is vacant.
> 
> "It's that time again!" Dardew announces to the devils above. "Are you ready for another game of Fiend Feud?" The audience cheers. "Contestants, take your places." Dardew clears his throat and gestures to the empty podium.
^0e8

This challenge is called Fiend Feud, a trivia game of brains and bloodshed where two teams compete for fame, fortune—and their lives. The opposing team consists of five [Cultists of Demogorgon](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/humanoid/cultist-of-demogorgon-wtthc.md) who are [Hostile](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/hostile-attitude-xphb.md) toward the characters. Dardew acts as host.

![](3-Mechanics/CLI/adventures/stranger-things-welcome-to-the-hellfire-club/img/074-cultist-sticker.webp#center)

Once the characters take their places behind the podium, the game begins.

- **Playing Fiend Feud.** Fiend Feud is a trivia game with three rounds and a mostly arbitrary points system. The game's rules are as follows:  
- **Trivia Questions.** Each round, Dardew asks the characters one question from the Fiend Feud Questions table, rerolling duplicates. These questions test player knowledge as much as character knowledge. The player characters are allowed to briefly deliberate before giving their response. If they answer the question correctly, their team receives 1 point.  
- **Chance to Steal.** If the characters answer a question incorrectly, the opposing team has a chance answer the question, receiving 1 point if they answer it correctly. The cultists are well-versed in occult lore and answer any such questions correctly, but they know little about anything else and get those questions wrong. The cultists know the answers to questions 1, 4, and 6 in the Fiend Feud Questions table.  
- **Bonus Round.** After three rounds of trivia, both teams can earn bonus points by killing members of the opposing team. Have everyone roll [Initiative](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/initiative-xphb.md). For each opposing team member they slay, a team earns 1 point.  

The game ends when only one team remains.

> 
> If you really want to run a certain encounter, you can just have whichever portal the characters go through lead to that area. It's an old DM trick I like to call **"the invisible railroad."**
^0e9

At the DM's discretion, a character can take the [Study](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/actions.md#Study) action to make a DC 15 Intelligence ([Arcana](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Arcana) or [Religion](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Religion)) check to know the answer to a question, learning the correct answer on a successful check.

##### Treasure

Characters receive treasure as follows:

- **Correct Answers.** Whenever a character correctly answers a question, the audience throws 10 GP into this area.  
- **Slaying Cultists.** Whenever a character slays a cultist, the audience throws 25 GP into this area.  
- **Winning the Game.** If the characters win Fiend Feud, the audience showers them with 150 GP.

##### Flail

The spinning flail is embedded in the ground between the gnolls. Each round on Initiative count 10 (losing Initiative ties), the flail's three heads crash to the ground. Each creature within 10 feet of the flail must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take `3d6` Bludgeoning damage and have the [Prone](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Prone) condition. The flail doesn't harm the gnolls.

The flail has AC 18, HP 40, and Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage. If the flail is destroyed, it stops spinning, and the audience roars in jubilation.

##### Treasure

Characters receive treasure as follows:

- **Defeating Gnolls.** Each time a character kills a gnoll, the audience throws 100 GP into this area.  
- **Destroying the Flail.** If a character destroys the flail, the audience throws 200 GP into this area.  

#### H5: Infernal Court

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> A judge with a nest of snakes for hair and wearing crimson robes presides over this infernal court. Two short podiums rest before the judge's bench, an imposing monument of black marble flanked by two snarling hounds; flames drip from the hounds' jaws. A lone imp perches atop one of the podiums. It snickers as you enter.
> 
> Nine petrified jurors with frightened expressions fill a juror's box to the north. The judge calls the court to order. "Order in the court!" the judge demands. "The accused have finally decided to join us—late and without representation. How do you plead?"
^0f5

The judge is Yudith Duboix, a no-nonsense Medusa. Judge Deboix is [Hostile](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/hostile-attitude-xphb.md) toward the characters, who have just entered their own trial. Two Hell Hounds ferociously obey the judge's commands.

![](3-Mechanics/CLI/adventures/stranger-things-welcome-to-the-hellfire-club/img/077-medusa-sticker.webp#center)

The trial proceeds through arraignment, statements, and sentencing, as detailed in the following sections. The characters can either represent themselves or allow the [Imp Trickster](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/imp-trickster-wtthc.md), an unlicensed attorney, to provide them with bad-faith representation.

The jury is composed of adventurers petrified by Judge Deboix. If the characters try to fight the court instead, they might be similarly turned to stone, in which case Dardew can amend his contract (see "Infernal Contract" earlier in this adventure) to also remove the [Petrified](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Petrified) condition.

- **Arraignment.** After the characters' plea, Judge Duboix summarizes the charges against them, which might include but aren't limited to the following:  
- **Arson.** A character is charged with arson if they've ever set anything on fire, including torches.  
- **Failure to Appear.** A character is charged with failure to appear if their player has ever arrived late to a game session.  
- **Murder.** A character is charged with murder if they've ever slain a creature in combat.  
- **Thievery.** A character is charged with thievery if they've ever stolen property, including gold or items found in a dungeon or taken from the dead.  
- **Trespassing.** A character is charged with trespassing if they've ever entered a location, including a dungeon, without permission.  

At your discretion, other offenses might be worthy of a charge in this kangaroo court.

##### Statements

The characters can confer and give a statement in their defense. A character can try to convince Judge Duboix of the party's innocence and make a DC 15 Charisma ([Persuasion](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Persuasion)) check. On a successful check, Judge Duboix finds the party not guilty and dismisses the characters from the court. On a failed check (or if the characters chose to let the imp represent them), Judge Duboix rules against the party and moves to sentencing.

If the party is found not guilty, the challenge is considered complete, and the portal to area H1 reopens.

![Judge Yudith Duboix, a stone-cold medusa judge, demands order in the infernal court](3-Mechanics/CLI/adventures/stranger-things-welcome-to-the-hellfire-club/img/078-04-03-judge-duboix.webp#center)

##### Sentencing

Judge Duboix's sentencing is harsh and swift. If the party is found guilty, the medusa sentences the characters to 1,000 years in prison. The judge then orders the hell hounds to escort the characters to the infernal prison (area H6) for detainment. If the characters refuse, the hell hounds leap into battle. Judge Duboix defends itself as necessary but otherwise abstains from combat. If both hounds are slain, Judge Duboix surrenders and overturns the previous verdict.

![](3-Mechanics/CLI/adventures/stranger-things-welcome-to-the-hellfire-club/img/079-hellhound-sticker.webp#center)

If the characters comply with sentencing and later return to this area, the courtroom is empty, and the portal to area H1 reopens.

##### Treasure

Characters receive treasure as follows:

- **Defeating Hell Hounds.** Each time a character kills a hound, the audience throws 200 GP into this area.  
- **Defeating Judge Duboix.** If Judge Duboix dies, the audience throws 2,000 GP into this area.  
- **Innocent Verdict.** If the party is found innocent, the audience throws 100 GP into this area.  

#### H6: Infernal Prison

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> A devil covered in sharp barbs patrols this open-air cell block. The devil rattles its tail against the bars of the six cages, laughing cruelly at the prisoners. A key hangs around the devil's neck.
^0fa

The warden of this prison is a masochistic Barbed Devil named Khaos, the Bringer of Pain. Khaos is [Hostile](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/hostile-attitude-xphb.md) toward the characters. When they enter, the devil grunts and points them to the biggest cage (cage 1), attacking any who refuse to enter. Khaos then returns to marching up and down the block.

![](3-Mechanics/CLI/adventures/stranger-things-welcome-to-the-hellfire-club/img/080-barbed-devil-sticker.webp#center)

Khaos enjoys catching escaped prisoners and breaking their spirits and bodies. The devil doesn't confiscate inmates' equipment and pretends not to notice if they try to free themselves or one another. Khaos doesn't pursue runaways beyond this area.

##### Cages

Khaos wears the key to these six locked cages. As a [Utilize](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/actions.md#Utilize) action, a character can try to pick a cage's lock using [Thieves' Tools](3-Mechanics/CLI/items/thieves-tools-xphb.md), doing so with a successful DC 15 Dexterity ([Sleight of Hand](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Sleight%20of%20Hand)) check. The check is made with [Disadvantage](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/disadvantage-xphb.md) if the character is trying to pick the lock from inside the cage.

Alternatively, a character can try to goad Khaos into unlocking their cage. As an [Influence](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/actions.md#Influence) action, a character can taunt the barbed devil and make a DC 13 Charisma ([Performance](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Performance)) check. On a successful check, Khaos unlocks the door, enters the cage, and strikes that character to teach them a lesson.

The cage's contents are as follows:

![](3-Mechanics/CLI/adventures/stranger-things-welcome-to-the-hellfire-club/img/081-imp-sticker.webp#center)

- **Cage 1** contains only a bench chained to the wall.  
- **Cage 2** contains a [Demogorgon Spawn](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/demogorgon-spawn-wtthc.md) captured on the battlefields of Avernus. The spawn is [Hostile](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/hostile-attitude-xphb.md) toward all other creatures and tries to tear them limb from limb if freed from its cell.  
- **Cage 3** is empty.  
- **Cage 4** contains a human [Cultist of Demogorgon](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/humanoid/cultist-of-demogorgon-wtthc.md) who warns any who address her that the "Sibilant Beast" will consume them all. The [Hostile](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/hostile-attitude-xphb.md) cultist fights any who enter her cell.  
- **Cage 5** contains an [Imp Trickster](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/imp-trickster-wtthc.md) playing a harmonica. The imp is [Indifferent](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/indifferent-attitude-xphb.md) toward the characters but aids them in combat in this area if freed.  
- **Cage 6** is empty.  

If a character escapes their cage or frees one of the other imprisoned creatures, Khaos brays with malevolent laughter and fights the escapee.

##### Treasure

Characters receive treasure as follows:

- **Defeating Khaos.** If a character kills Khaos, the audience throws 2,000 GP into this area.  
- **Defeating the Demogorgon Spawn.** If a character kills the [Demogorgon Spawn](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/demogorgon-spawn-wtthc.md), the audience throws 150 GP into this area.

It stretches its wide, leathery wings and unfurls its heads to reveal two gaping maws of toothed, fleshy petals. The behemoth bellows two bone-chilling roars.

The final challenge in the Hellfire Games is the [Demodragon](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/demodragon-wtthc.md), an abomination that has slaughtered scores of contestants. ## Conclusion

The Hellfire Games conclude when the characters vanquish the Demodragon or die trying. If the characters defeat the Demodragon, read or paraphrase the following text:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> With an earthshaking crash, the Demodragon collapses dead before you. The crowd goes wild.
> 
> Dardew soars overhead. "Wow! What a show!" he crows. "Friends and fiends, please join me in welcoming our latest Hellfire champions!"
> 
> He flies toward you and says quietly, "Adventurers, now would be a great time to take a bow. You've earned it."
^101

In addition to the treasure they collected in the Hellfire Dome, the characters earn the following rewards for winning the Hellfire Games:

- **Hellfire Club Membership.** Dardew invites characters to join the Hellfire Club. If they accept his invitation, they each receive a pendant worth 50 GP. The pendant depicts the face of a red devil flanked by a flail and flaming sword.  
- **Hellfire Hall of Fame.** The characters are inducted into the Hellfire Hall of Fame. Their names are recorded forever in the annals of infernal history alongside other legendary heroes and villains.  
- **Voiding the Infernal Contract.** If any character entered into an infernal contract with Dardew (see "Infernal Contract" earlier in this adventure), Dardew voids the contract.  

When the characters are ready to leave the Nine Hells, Dardew magically returns them to Greyhawkins. Before he departs, Dardew thanks them for the magnificent performance.
