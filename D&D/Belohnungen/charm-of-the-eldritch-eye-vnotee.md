# Charm of the Eldritch Eye

You can cast [Clairvoyance](3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/clairvoyance-xphb.md) as an action, without using a spell slot and requiring no material components. Once used three times, the charm vanishes.

*Source: Vecna: Nest of the Eldritch Eye*
