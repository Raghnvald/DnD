# Charm of the Fates

You can tug on the threads of fate to tweak circumstance in your favor. After you make an ability check, an attack roll, or a saving throw, you can roll a `dice:d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`d10`) and add it to the total, potentially turning failure into success. Once used three times, the charm vanishes from you.

*Source: The Book of Many Things p. 62*
