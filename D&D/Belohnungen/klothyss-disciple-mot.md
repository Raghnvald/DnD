# Klothys's Disciple
*Piety Trait*  

*Piety 25+ Klothys trait*

You develop a second sense, allowing you to intuit the tug and pull of destiny. You can't be surprised, provided you aren't [incapacitated](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Incapacitated).

*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 58*
