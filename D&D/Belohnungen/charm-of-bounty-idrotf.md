# Charm of Bounty

> [!note]
> This charm can be bestowed by a [chwinga](/3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/elemental/chwinga-toa.md) living in Icewind Dale or other cold regions by using their Magical Gift action.

This charm has 3 charges. As an action, you can expend 1 of the charm's charges to cast the create food and water spell, requiring no components. Once all its charges have been expended, this charm vanishes from you.

*Source: Icewind Dale: Rime of the Frostmaiden p. 283*
