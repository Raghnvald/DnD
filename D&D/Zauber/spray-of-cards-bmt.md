# Spray of Cards
*2nd-level, Conjuration*  

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (15-foot Cone)
- **Components:** V, S, M (a deck of cards)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You spray a 15-foot cone of spectral cards. Each creature in that area must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes `dice:2d10|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d10`) force damage and has the blinded condition until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage only.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by `dice:1d10|noform|noparens|avg|text(1d10)` for each slot level above 2nd.

*Source: The Book of Many Things p. 50*
