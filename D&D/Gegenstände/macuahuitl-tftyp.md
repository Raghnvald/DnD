# Macuahuitl
*Weapon (longsword)*  


- **Damage**:
  - One-handed: 1d8 slashing
  - Two-handed: 1d10 slashing
- **Properties**: Versatile
- **Weight**: 3.0 lbs.

You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this longsword, which is made of laminated wood, and inset with jagged teeth of obsidian. It deals an extra `dice:2d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`2d6`) damage to any creature of the plant type.

*Source: Tales from the Yawning Portal p. 70*
