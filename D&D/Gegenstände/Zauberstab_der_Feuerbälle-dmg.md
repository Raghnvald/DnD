# Wand of Fireballs
*Wand, major, rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)*  
![](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/img/wand-of-fireballs.webp#right)

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

This wand has 7 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast the [fireball](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/fireball.md) spell (save DC 15) from it. For 1 charge, you cast the 3rd-level version of the spell. You can increase the spell slot level by one for each additional charge you expend.

The wand regains `1d6 + 1` expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the wand's last charge, roll a `d20`. On a 1, the wand crumbles into ashes and is destroyed.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 210. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>*
