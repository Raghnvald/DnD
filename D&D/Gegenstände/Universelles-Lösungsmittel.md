# Universal Solvent
*Wondrous Item, minor, legendary*  

- **Cost**: ⏤
- **Weight**: ⏤

This tube holds milky liquid with a strong alcohol smell. You can use an action to pour the contents of the tube onto a surface within reach. The liquid instantly dissolves up to 1 square foot of adhesive it touches, including [sovereign glue](Ewiger_Leim-old.md).

*Source: SRD / Basic Rules*
