# Boots of Haste
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*  



While you wear these boots, you can click your heels together to cast the [haste](/3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/haste-xphb.md) spell on yourself as a bonus action. You don't suffer from lethargy when the spell ends when cast using these boots. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until next dawn.

*Source: Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting Reborn p. 194*
