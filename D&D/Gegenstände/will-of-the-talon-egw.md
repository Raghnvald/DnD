# Will of the Talon
*Weapon ([war pick](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/war-pick-xphb.md)), artifact (requires attunement)*

- **Properties**: [Vestige of Divergence](/3-Mechanics/CLI/item-properties.md#Vestige%20of%20Divergence)
