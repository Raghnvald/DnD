# Map or Scroll Case
*Adventuring gear*  

- **Cost**: 1 gp
- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

This cylindrical leather case can hold up to ten rolled-up [sheets of paper](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/paper-one-sheet.md) or five rolled-up [sheets of parchment](/3-Mechanics/CLI/items/parchment-one-sheet.md).
