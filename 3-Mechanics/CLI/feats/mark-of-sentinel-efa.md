# Mark of Sentinel
*Source: Eberron: Forge of the Artificer p. 41*  

**Prerequisite**: Eberron Campaign, Can't Have Another D Feat

You gain the following benefits.

**Sentinel's Intuition.** When you make a Wisdom (Insight or Perception) check, you can roll `1d4` and add the number rolled to the ability check.

**Guardian's Shield.** You always have the Shield spell prepared. You can cast it once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>. You can also cast it using any spell slots you have. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell (choose when you select this feat).

**Vigilant Guardian.** When a creature you can see within 5 feet of you is hit by an attack roll, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to swap places with that creature, and you are hit by the attack instead. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Proficiency Bonus</span> and regain all expended uses when you finish a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span>.

**Spells of the Mark.** If you have the Spellcasting or Pact Magic class feature, the spells on the Mark of Sentinel Spells table are added to that feature's spell list.
