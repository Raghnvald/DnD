# Boon of Blazing Dawn
*Source: Astarion's Book of Hungers p. 11*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Beloved of the Sun.** You have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Immunity</span> to Radiant damage.

**Blazing Strike.** When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, the damage can be Radiant or the weapon's normal damage type (your choice).

**Burst of Sunlight.** Once per turn when you hit a creature with an attack that deals Radiant damage, you can emit <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bright Light</span> in a 30-foot radius from yourself and <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Dim Light</span> for an additional 30 feet until the start of your next turn. This light is sunlight.
