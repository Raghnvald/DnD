# Dirge
*6th-level, Enchantment*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Self (60-foot Emanation)
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Deathly power fills a 60-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> originating from you for the duration.

When you cast this spell, you can designate creatures to be unaffected by it. Any other creature can't regain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> while in the <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span>. Whenever the <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> enters a creature's space and whenever a creature enters the <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Emanation</span> or ends its turn there, the creature makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes `3d10` Necrotic damage and has the Prone condition. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and its <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Speed</span> is halved. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

## Casting as a Circle Spell

Casting this as a Circle spell requires a minimum of two secondary casters. If the spell is cast as a Circle spell, its duration becomes Concentration, up to 10 minutes.

A creature that fails its save against the spell's effect also gains 1 Exhaustion level. While the creature has Exhaustion levels, finishing a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Long Rest</span> neither restores lost <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> nor reduces the creature's Exhaustion level.

When the spell is cast, each secondary caster must expend a level 4+ spell slot; otherwise, the spell fails.
