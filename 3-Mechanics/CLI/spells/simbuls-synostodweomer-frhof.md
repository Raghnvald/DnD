# Simbul's Synostodweomer
*7th-level, Transmutation*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 hour

You imbue one creature you touch with magical healing energy for the duration. Whenever the target casts a spell using a spell slot, the target can immediately roll a number of unexpended <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Point Dice</span> equal to the spell slot's level and regain <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Hit Points</span> equal to the roll's total plus your spellcasting ability modifier; those dice are then expended.

*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 145*
