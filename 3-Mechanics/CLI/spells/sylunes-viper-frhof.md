# Syluné's Viper
*3rd-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a snake fang)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

A shimmering, spectral snake encircles your body for the duration. You gain 15 <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span>; the spell ends early if you have no <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> left.

While the spell is active, you gain the following benefits:

- **Climbing.** You gain a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Climb Speed</span> equal to your <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Speed</span>.  
- **Venomous Bite.** As a Magic action, you can make a ranged spell attack using the snake against one creature within 50 feet. On a hit, the target takes `1d6` Force damage and has the Poisoned condition until the start of your next turn. While Poisoned, the target has the Incapacitated condition.  

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** For each spell slot level above 3, the number of <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Temporary Hit Points</span> you gain from this spell increases by 5, and the damage of Venomous Bite increases by `1d6`.

*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 147*
