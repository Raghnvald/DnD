# Death Armor
*2nd-level, Necromancy*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (an onyx worth 50+ GP, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

For the duration, an inky aura surrounds one creature you touch. The target has <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Advantage</span> on <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Death Saving Throws</span>, and once per turn, when a creature within 5 feet of the target hits it with a melee attack roll, the attacker takes `2d4` Necrotic damage.

*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 143*
