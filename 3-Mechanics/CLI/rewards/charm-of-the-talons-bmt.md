# Charm of the Talons

As an action, you can cast Dispel Magic, requiring no spell components and using your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as the spellcasting ability (your choice). If you successfully end any spells with it, you gain `dice:1d6|noform|noparens|avg` (`1d6`) "temporary hit points" for each spell level of the highest-level spell ended. Once used three times, the charm vanishes from you.

*Source: The Book of Many Things p. 62*
