# Manifest Charm of Daanvi, the Perfect Order
*Charm*  

You can expend the Charm (no action required) to end the Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, or Poisoned condition on yourself.

*Source: Eberron: Forge of the Artificer p. 50*
