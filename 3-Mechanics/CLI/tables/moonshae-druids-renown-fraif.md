# Moonshae Druids Renown
*Source: Forgotten Realms: Adventures in Faerûn p. 185* 

**Moonshae Druids Renown**

| Renown Score | Reward |
|--------------|--------|
| 3+ | Moonshae druids and their allies are <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span> to you by default. |
| 5+ | You can purchase Potions of Healing from the druids for half cost. |
| 10+ | Beasts in the Moonshaes are <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span> to you by default. |
| 15+ | You can purchase Spell Scrolls containing Druid spells of level 5 or lower from the druids. |
| 20+ | You are allowed to visit moonwells guarded by the druids and participate in their ceremonies. Plant creatures in the Moonshaes are <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Friendly</span> to you by default. |
| 30 | The first time your Renown Score reaches 30, the druids offer to take you to a moonwell and cast the Commune spell—contacting the Earthmother—on your behalf. |
