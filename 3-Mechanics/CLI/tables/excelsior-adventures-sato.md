# Excelsior Adventures
*Source: Sigil and the Outlands p. 71* 

**Excelsior Adventures**

| dice: d4 | Adventure Hook |
|----------|----------------|
| 1 | An [androsphinx](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/celestial/sphinx-of-valor-xmm.md) arrives in Excelsior and declares itself the new high chancellor. Forough petitions the characters to help cement her authority. |
| 2 | The [empyrean](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/miscellaneous/empyrean-xmm.md) Tygrant receives a prophecy that he can return to Mount Celestia only if he is humbled through defeat. He challenges all of Excelsior, including the characters, to wrestling matches in his tower. |
| 3 | Allowed into the "forum" on an ancient contract, a silver-tongued [pit fiend](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/pit-fiend-xmm.md) invites Excelsior's orators to debate the worth of a soul. An angel asks the characters to take the podium. |
| 4 | The faith of a divine champion ([gladiator](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/humanoid/gladiator-xmm.md)) begins to waver in the "Chandelier", causing their cloud keep to shudder. A lantern archon (see "Morte's Planar Parade") asks the characters to erase the warrior's doubt before their fortress falls from the sky. |
