# The Vanishing Gnome
*Source: Stranger Things: Welcome to the Hellfire Club* 

*An Adventure for Level 1 Characters*

![](3-Mechanics/CLI/adventures/stranger-things-welcome-to-the-hellfire-club/img/002-01-05-vanishing-gnome-cover.webp#center)

This adventure is designed to fill one or two sessions of play.

If you aren't using the Map of Greyhawkins, it can take place in any shadowy dungeon.

## Key Plot Points

This information is key to the adventure's story:

- **Plot Point 1.** [Flizzlebin](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/flizzlebin-wtthc.md) magically contacts the characters and challenges them to find him in the Dungeon of Shadows.  
- **Plot Point 2.** The characters navigate the Dungeon of Shadows in search of Flizzlebin, who calls out to them from his hiding place in a twisted reflection of the dungeon connected to a mysterious realm known as the Vale of Shadows.  
- **Plot Point 3.** A spawn of Demogorgon, a demon lord, has escaped the Vale of Shadows. The characters first encounter the creature in the Dungeon of Shadows, but it likely escapes.  
- **Plot Point 4.** After defeating the [Demogorgon Spawn](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/demogorgon-spawn-wtthc.md), the characters find Flizzlebin and earn the gnome's reward.

## Adventure Background

[Flizzlebin](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/flizzlebin-wtthc.md), the Vanishing Gnome, tests the mettle of would-be heroes in dangerous games of hide-and-seek in dungeons throughout the land. Over the years, the elusive gnome has magically contacted countless travelers, but few have accepted his quests—and fewer have completed them. Successful seekers have emerged from these dungeons with priceless treasures and pockets brimming with gold.

Recently, Flizzlebin vanished to the Dungeon of Shadows, a maze that borders a gloomy, monster-infested realm. There, his calls to adventurers awakened a foul creature: a spawn of the demon lord Demogorgon! As Flizzlebin eagerly broadcasts his quest, the ravenous demon stalks the dungeon, complicating the gnome's game and endangering all who seek him.

## Beginning the Adventure

When the players are ready to begin the adventure, read or paraphrase the following text:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> You travel a well-trod road through craggy and forested country. As you pass a dense thicket, a voice calls out to you exuberantly.
> 
> "Well-met, adventurers! 'Tis I, Flizzlebin, the Vanishing Gnome, the illustrious vagabond, the emissary of evasion! I have sequestered myself deep within the fabled Dungeon of Shadows, where I wait with a magical reward for those who prove themselves worthy. Find me, and the treasure is yours. Travel east to find the dungeon's entrance. Beware! Horrid creatures lurk within the dungeon's darkest corners... and so do I."
> 
> The voice fades. Do you accept this quest?
^019

At your discretion, one or more of the characters might have heard of Flizzlebin from stories shared in taverns throughout the land. If the characters follow the gnome's instructions, they quickly arrive at the Dungeon of Shadows.

## Dungeon of Shadows

The Dungeon of Shadows is the bridge between Greyhawkins and the Vale of Shadows, a mirror world in a realm of shadow. Bloodthirsty gnolls, giant arachnids, deadly traps, and worse terrors await visitors to the dungeon.

A warped version of the dungeon is reflected within the Vale of Shadows. Flizzlebin hides within this shadow of the dungeon above, which is accessible only through portals hidden throughout the dungeon.

### Arriving at the Dungeon

When the characters arrive at the Dungeon of Shadows, read or paraphrase the following text:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> You reach the entrance to the Dungeon of Shadows. Black briars grow around the entrance's mouth, which resembles a howling stone face. Past the opening, rough-hewn stairs descend into darkness.
^01d

A character can inspect the entrance and make one of the following checks to recall lore about the dungeon and the gloomy realm it borders:

**DC 12 Intelligence ([History](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#History)).** Monsters infest this dungeon, which is suffused with shadowy magic. The dungeon was once a temple devoted to a long-forgotten god, and its reputation is as tenebrous as its entrance. Rumors speak of a second, more sinister dungeon connected to this one.

**DC 12 Intelligence ([Arcana](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Arcana)).** The dungeon is magically linked to the Vale of Shadows, a grim reflection of the natural world. Dreadful creatures and living shadows lurk in this dark and twisted reality; occasionally, some of these creatures escape the vale to plague the people of Greyhawkins.

![](3-Mechanics/CLI/adventures/stranger-things-welcome-to-the-hellfire-club/img/007-01-30-test-sticker.webp#center)

The staircase descends 60 feet to area D1. When the characters enter the dungeon, use the Dungeon of Shadows Map to see where the characters begin in that area.

> [!note] 
> 
> Flizzlebin can speak to the characters whenever you want him to. If your players are really green, he can be the voice of the DM, A.K.A. the voice of reason. He might contact the characters to drop a hint, give words of encouragement... or warn them of **CERTAIN DEATH**.
^01e

- **DM Secret!.** The map for this adventure depicts the Dungeon of Shadows and its reflection in the Vale of Shadows. Keep the Vale of Shadows hidden from your players until they enter it, as detailed later in this adventure.  

### Dungeon Features

The dungeon has the following features:

- **Ceilings.** The ceilings are 20 feet high in rooms and 10 feet high in passages.  
- **Doors and Secret Doors.** Doors are unlocked and made of basalt. Secret doors manifest as spidery, elastic fissures that blend in with their surroundings. As a [Search](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/actions.md#Search) action, a character can examine a wall that contains a secret door and make a DC 15 Wisdom ([Perception](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Perception)) check, noticing it on a successful check. A secret door opens as easily as any normal door.  
- **Lighting.** Torches in iron wall sconces fill the dungeon with [Bright Light](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/bright-light-xphb.md).

The gnome magically contacts the characters as they explore the dungeon, but he doesn't say anything else to characters in this area unless you decide otherwise. Flizzlebin has discovered that his communications have attracted the spawn of Demogorgon (see area D3), and he doesn't want to further draw its ire.

- Door to D3 This door bears a relief of a ferocious, many-horned minotaur above a stone maze. The inscription reads "Labyrinth of Doom."  
- Door to D4 In this door's relief, three mischievous creatures with pointed ears and sharp teeth surround an anvil. The inscription reads, "Forge of Gibbletrax."

This room holds refuse discarded by the dungeon's denizens.

##### Treasure

As a [Search](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/actions.md#Search) action, a character can sift through the trash and make a DC 12 Wisdom ([Perception](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Perception)) check. The first character to succeed on the check unearths five ivory urns. Each urn is worth 10 GP. Four of the urns are empty, but one contains a [Potion of Healing](3-Mechanics/CLI/items/potion-of-healing-xdmg.md) and a tiny pewter statue of a two-headed demon worth 90 GP. A character who inspects the statue and succeeds on a DC 13 Intelligence ([Religion](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Religion)) check recognizes the statue as an idol of the demon lord Demogorgon.

#### D3: Labyrinth of Doom

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> The torches along the walls of this maze begin to flicker, and a familiar disembodied voice says, "Many adventurers have met their demise in these corridors. Beware the fearsome minotaur!"
^028

![A spawn of Demogorgon feeds on its latest victim](3-Mechanics/CLI/adventures/stranger-things-welcome-to-the-hellfire-club/img/012-00-02-demogorgon-feeds-dm-v1.webp#center)

Until recently, a bloodthirsty minotaur ruled this trap-filled labyrinth. However, an even more vicious creature—the spawn of Demogorgon, a demon lord—escaped the Vale of Shadows and slew the minotaur. The spawn is devouring the minotaur's corpse in the middle of the labyrinth.

##### Spear Traps

The labyrinth contains three spear traps triggered by pressure plates hidden in the floor, as denoted with a "T" on the Dungeon of Shadows map. Cobwebs and dust obscure the holes that house these spears.

Each spear trap has the following rules:

- **Detect and Disarm.** As a [Search](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/actions.md#Search) action, a character can examine the walls and make a DC 15 Wisdom ([Perception](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Perception)) check, detecting the holes on a successful check. Alternatively, a character can take the [Study](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/actions.md#Study) action to inspect the floor and make a DC 15 Intelligence ([Investigation](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Investigation)) check, detecting the pressure plate on a successful check.  
- **Triggering the Trap.** When a creature moves onto a trap's pressure plate for the first time on a turn, spears thrust out from the walls. Each creature on the pressure plate must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity Saving Throw or be struck by a spear, taking `1d6` Piercing damage. The spears then retract, and the trap resets immediately.  

##### Terror in the Maze

When the characters reach the center of the maze, read or paraphrase the following text:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> The torches in this bloodstained corridor sputter erratically. A thin, sinewy creature with slimy, gray skin and elongated limbs crouches over a slain minotaur. The corpse squelches and snaps unsettlingly as the creature gorges on it. A small chest lies beside the mangled minotaur.
^02b

The [Demogorgon Spawn](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/fiend/demogorgon-spawn-wtthc.md) continues devouring the minotaur for 1 minute or until it detects the characters. The spawn is [Hostile](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/hostile-attitude-xphb.md) toward all other creatures.

> [!note] 
> 
> This encounter is pretty tough. Go easy on them... unless they're being ********, in which case you should give 'em hell.
^02c

To sneak past the spawn while it feeds, a character must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity ([Stealth](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Stealth)) check. On a failed check, or if the characters otherwise disturb the spawn, it turns toward the adventurers. When that happens, read or paraphrase the following text:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> The creature turns toward you, and its faceless head peels back to reveal five toothed, fleshy petals around a gaping mouth. It screeches and rushes toward you.
> 
> Roll Initiative!
^02d

The Demogorgon spawn fights until it is reduced to 25 Hit Points or fewer, at which point it retreats through the secret door to area S3 of the Shadow Dungeon (detailed later in this adventure). If the characters manage to defeat the Demogorgon spawn here, the adventure doesn't end; the characters still must find Flizzlebin to complete his quest.

If the characters don't disturb the spawn and aren't detected, the creature finishes its meal and then relocates to area S4 in the Shadow Dungeon.

##### Secret Door

A secret door (see "Dungeon Features") is set into the wall in the southeast corner of the labyrinth. It leads to area S3. Characters who witnessed the Demogorgon spawn use this secret door automatically succeed on checks to find the door.

#### D4: Forge of Gibbletrax

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> Clanging hammers ring out in this sweltering chamber. Three goblin smiths cluster around a glowing forge in the center of the room. The goblins sing in scratchy tones as they work:
> 
> *"Deep within the Dungeon of Shadows*,
> 
> *We strengthen steel to slay the beasts.*
> 
> *Upside down in the Vale of Shadows*,
> 
> *Brave heroes delve to keep us free."*
^031

Blessed by a long-forgotten god, the Forge of Gibbletrax is a magic forge stewarded by three Goblin Warriors named Ble, Gib, and Trax. These smiths craft gear to defend against the dungeon's threats.

The goblins are initially [Friendly](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/friendly-attitude-xphb.md) toward the characters but defend themselves if necessary. If the characters approach the goblins peacefully, the goblins greet the characters warmly and ask what brings them to the dungeon.

Ble, Glib, and Trax usually keep to themselves, but they recently clashed with the gnolls in area D6, whose profane rituals threaten the sanctity of the forge. If the characters agree to end the gnolls' foul rites, the goblins vow to imbue the characters' weapons with the forge's magic.

##### Forge of Gibbletrax

The goblins' magical forge can temporarily infuse weapons and ammunition with immense heat. As a Bonus Action, a character can dip one weapon or piece of ammunition into the forge, coating it in a thin layer of molten ore. A creature that takes damage from the ore-coated weapon or ammunition takes an extra `1d6` Fire damage. Once coated, the weapon retains its heat for 10 minutes or until its damage is dealt, whichever comes first.

A character can benefit from the Forge of Gibbletrax only once, unless the goblins permit them to do so again. If all three goblins are slain or the forge is removed from this area, its magic ceases to function.

##### Crystal Spider

The crystal spider is a Medium object with AC 13, HP 50, Resistance to Piercing and Slashing damage, and Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage. Destroying the crystal spider frees the treasure inside (detailed below).

##### Secret Door

Set into the east wall of this room is a secret door (see "Dungeon Features") that connects to area S5.

##### Statue

The statue depicts a knight in a gallant pose. As a [Study](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/actions.md#Study) action, a character can examine the statue and make a DC 12 Intelligence ([Investigation](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Investigation)) check. On a successful check, the character realizes the statue's base is unstable; a significant push could topple the statue.

As an action, a character within 5 feet of the statue can make a DC 10 Strength ([Athletics](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Athletics)) check to topple the statue into a space within 5 feet of it, doing so on a successful check. When the statue topples, each creature beneath it takes `4d6` Bludgeoning damage and has the [Prone](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Prone) condition. The statue crumbles on impact, revealing a treasure hidden inside it (see "Treasure" below).

##### Webs

The web-filled areas are [Difficult Terrain](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/difficult-terrain-xphb.md).

A creature that enters a web-filled area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity Saving Throw or have the [Restrained](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) condition. As an action, a creature Restrained by the webs can try to escape from them, doing so with a successful DC 12 Strength ([Athletics](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Athletics)) or Dexterity ([Acrobatics](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Acrobatics)) check.

![](3-Mechanics/CLI/adventures/stranger-things-welcome-to-the-hellfire-club/img/016-flame-tongue-greatsword-sticker.webp#center)

Each 5-foot [Cube](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/cube-area-of-effect-xphb.md) of webs has AC 10; HP 7; Vulnerability to Fire damage; and Immunity to Piercing, Poison, and Psychic damage.

##### Treasure

Inside the statue is a heart-shaped emerald worth 30 GP. The sword encased in the crystal spider is a [Flame Tongue (Greatsword)](3-Mechanics/CLI/items/flame-tongue-xdmg.md).

### Shadow Dungeon

Beneath the Dungeon of Shadows lies the Shadow Dungeon, a gloomy reflection of the dungeon that borders the Vale of Shadows. Though its chambers mirror the areas above, their contents differ, distorted by the eerie realm they border.

#### Finding Flizzlebin

To find Flizzlebin, the characters must defeat the Demogorgon spawn and any [Hostile](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/hostile-attitude-xphb.md) creatures in Flizzlebin's hiding spot (see the "Hide-and-Seek" section earlier in this adventure). When both conditions are met, the gnome appears to the characters; proceed to this adventure's conclusion.

If the characters reach Flizzlebin's hiding spot before they vanquish the Demogorgon spawn, read or paraphrase the following text:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> "Ah! You found my hiding spot! But your work isn't done yet," says a familiar voice. "To claim your prize, you must slay the horrid creature that hunts me."
^041

Flizzlebin doesn't bequeath his reward until the characters comply with his instructions. At your discretion, he might reveal the Demogorgon spawn's location—likely area S4.

### Shadow Dungeon Features

The Shadow Dungeon has the following features:

- **Ceilings.** The ceilings are 20 feet high in rooms and 10 feet high in passages.  
- **Lighting.** The dungeon is unlit. Area descriptions assume the characters have a light source or other means of seeing in [Darkness](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/darkness-xphb.md). Bare iron sconces line the walls.  
- **Doors and Secret Doors.** Doors are unlocked and made of basalt. Secret doors manifest as spidery, elastic fissures that blend in with their surroundings and exude faint rays of natural light when opened. As a [Search](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/actions.md#Search) action, a character can examine a wall that contains a secret door and make a DC 15 Wisdom ([Perception](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Perception)) check, noticing it on a successful check.  

### Shadow Dungeon Locations

The characters can enter and leave the Shadow Dungeon through the secret door that connects areas D3 and S3 or the secret door that connects areas D5 and S5. The following locations are keyed to the upside-down portion of the Dungeon of Shadows map.

#### S1: Chamber of Bad Choices

> [!readaloud]

^045

This room mirrors the Chamber of Choice (area D1). The corpse is that of a dwarf explorer who perished four days ago while searching for Flizzlebin. The dwarf was killed by an Undead shadow.

The dwarf's body is unnaturally thin, and its clothes look too big. A character can inspect the corpse and make a DC 12 Wisdom ([Medicine](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Medicine)) check. On a successful check, the character discerns the dwarf's life was sapped by a supernatural force.

The dwarf clutches a note. If the characters remove it from the corpse, show the players the Dungeon Warning Handout. After the characters read the note, a Shadow rises from the dwarf's corpse and begins combat.

##### Doors

Each of the five doors is adorned with a relief sculpture and an inscription as follows:

- Door to S2 (Urn). This door bears the image of an urn wrapped in ink-black vines. The inscription reads "Wasteland of Filthy Vines."  
- Door to S3 (Labyrinth). This door's relief depicts a fungal maze. The inscription reads, "Labyrinth of Gloom."  
- Door to S4 (Spiked Anvil). In this door's relief, a shadowy figure looms over a spiked anvil. The inscription reads, "Forge of Annihilation."  
- Door to S5 (Web). A black spiderweb is etched into this door. The inscription reads "Den of Bitsy."  
- Door to S6 (Hand and Eye). A desiccated hand holding an eye decorates this door. Deep scratches make the inscription illegible.  

##### Staircase

The staircase, blocked by rubble, leads to the Vale of Shadows. That realm and its contents are beyond the scope of this adventure, but you can flesh them out as you see fit. Otherwise, the staircase is impassible.

The first character to sift through the trash and succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom ([Perception](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Perception)) check unearths five cracked urns hewn from black granite. The urns are worthless. Four of the urns are empty, but one contains a [Potion of Healing](3-Mechanics/CLI/items/potion-of-healing-xdmg.md) and a glass eye with an emerald pupil worth 10 GP.

#### S3: Labyrinth of Gloom

##### Spore Traps

The labyrinth contains three spore traps—swollen plant bulbs that exhale puffs of toxic spores—triggered by stepping on pressure plates of inflated mushrooms. These traps are marked on Dungeon of Shadows map with a "T." The fungus-infested walls obscure the bulbs that spray these spores.

Each spore trap has the following rules:

- **Detect and Disarm.** As a [Study](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/actions.md#Study) action, a character can examine the walls and make a DC 15 Intelligence ([Nature](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Nature)) check, identifying the toxic bulbs on a successful check. Alternatively, a character can take the Study action to inspect the floor and make a DC 15 Intelligence ([Investigation](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Investigation)) check, identifying the fungal pressure plate on a successful check. Trimming the stems that connect the bulbs to the pressure plate prevents the trap from triggering.  
- **Triggering the Trap.** When a creature moves onto a pressure plate for the first time on a turn, the bulbs spray toxic spores that fill a 5-foot [Cube](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/cube-area-of-effect-xphb.md) centered on the pressure plate. Each creature in the Cube must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution Saving Throw or have the [Poisoned](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) condition for 1 hour. The trap resets immediately.  

##### Treasure

A dilapidated chest in the middle of the labyrinth contains a broken wand and 25 GP. The wand is useless.

##### Forge of Annihilation

Unlike the goblins' blessed forge in the dungeon above, this forge is a cursed apparatus capable only of destruction. The Forge of Annihilation causes living creatures to wither and die. As a [Study](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/actions.md#Study) action, a character can inspect the forge and make a DC 13 Intelligence ([Arcana](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Arcana)) check. On a successful check, the character identifies the eldritch hazard and the threat it poses.

A creature that enters the forge for the first time or starts its turn there must make a DC 15 Constitution Saving Throw, taking `4d8` Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

##### Secret Door

Set into the east wall in this room is a secret door (see "Shadow Dungeon Features") that connects to area D5.

##### Statue

The statue depicts a knight with a menacing helm. As a [Study](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/actions.md#Study) action, a character can examine the statue and make a DC 12 Intelligence ([Investigation](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Investigation)) check. On a successful check, the character realizes the statue's base is unstable; a significant push could topple the statue.

As an action, a character within 5 feet of the statue can make a DC 10 Strength ([Athletics](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Athletics)) check to topple the statue into a space within 5 feet of it, doing so on a successful check. When the statue topples, each creature beneath it takes `4d6` Bludgeoning damage and has the [Prone](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Prone) condition. The statue crumbles on impact, revealing treasure within (see "Treasure" below), but its helm remains intact.

##### Webs

The web-filled areas are [Difficult Terrain](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/difficult-terrain-xphb.md).

A creature that enters a web-filled area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity Saving Throw or have the [Restrained](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) condition. As an action, a creature Restrained by the webs can try to escape from them, doing so with a successful DC 12 Strength ([Athletics](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Athletics)) or Dexterity ([Acrobatics](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Acrobatics)) check.

Each 5-foot [Cube](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/cube-area-of-effect-xphb.md) of webs has AC 10; HP 7; Vulnerability to Fire damage; and Immunity to Piercing, Poison, and Psychic damage.

##### Treasure

The statue's helmet is a crimson [Dread Helm](3-Mechanics/CLI/items/dread-helm-wtthc.md) that resembles the head of a horned devil. Inside the statue is a heart-shaped black tourmaline worth 30 GP.

#### S6: Altar of Vecna

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> Low fog pervades this eerily quiet chamber. A stone altar rises from the center of the floor. Atop the altar, a dismembered hand grasps an hourglass half-filled with black sand and adorned with bone spindles. The sand defies gravity, flowing to the top bulb.
^05b

This room mirrors the Altar of Yeenoghu (area D6), but this altar honors evil of a viler caliber.

The first character to inspect the hourglass hears the sound of a ticking clock, followed by a baleful chime. The hourglass then cracks, and the sand spills out, revealing a mummified eye within the lower bulb. No other character hears the sound.

Afterward, a character can examine the altar's contents and make a DC 12 Intelligence ([Religion](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Religion)) check. On a successful check, the character recognizes the hand and the eye as the symbol of Vecna, a powerful lich feared throughout Greyhawkins. The hourglass is a ceremonial object used by members of Vecna's cult to symbolize the lich's dreaded—and inevitable—return.

## Conclusion

After the characters defeat the Demogorgon spawn and locate [Flizzlebin](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/npc/flizzlebin-wtthc.md), the gnome rewards them. Read or paraphrase the following:

> [!readaloud] 
> 
> A bearded gnome in a fancy cap addresses you enthusiastically. "Bravery, majesty, and teamwork!" he exclaims. "How wonderful it was to guide you on your quest." He removes his cap.
> 
> "For your heroism, I bestow on you my magic cap. But know this: The real treasures were the bonds you formed along the way.
> 
> "Go forth! Many great adventures await you."
^05d

For their efforts, Flizzlebin gives the characters his [Cap of Vanishing](3-Mechanics/CLI/items/cap-of-vanishing-wtthc.md). If one of the characters didn't find a magic item during the adventure, the gnome presents the cap to that character.

![](3-Mechanics/CLI/adventures/stranger-things-welcome-to-the-hellfire-club/img/023-cap-of-vanishing-sticker.webp#center)

Flizzlebin is [Friendly](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/friendly-attitude-xphb.md) and willing to speak to the characters. During conversation, he fondly recalls the characters' deeds and commends them on their creativity, heroism, or any other qualities you deem appropriate. He then wishes them farewell and vanishes once more, disappearing to a faraway dungeon to challenge a new group of adventurers.

### Character Advancement

After the characters receive Flizzlebin's reward, everyone in the party gains 1 level. Direct each player to record any treasure they found over the course of this adventure.
