# Bright Fungal Cloak
*Adventuring gear*  
![](3-Mechanics/CLI/items/img/bright-fungal-cloak.webp#right)

- **Cost**: 25 gp
- **Weight**: 4.0 lbs.

*Never spelunk in the dark again! This cloak of bioluminescent fungus fibers is sure to brighten your day.*

While wearing a Bright Fungal Cloak, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span> to furl or unfurl it. When the cloak is unfurled, it sheds <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bright Light</span> in a 5-foot radius and <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Dim Light</span> for an additional 5 feet.

One pound of fungus is sewn into a Bright Fungal Cloak. This fungus can be eaten as food. Once all the fungus is consumed, the cloak becomes a mundane set of Traveler's Clothes.

*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 132*
