# Cloak of Billowing
*Wondrous item, common*  
![](3-Mechanics/CLI/items/img/cloak-of-billowing-stranger-things.webp#right)


While wearing this cloak, you can take a [Bonus Action](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb.md) to make it billow dramatically for 1 minute.

## Special Use:

You have advantage on Charisma ([Performance](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/skills.md#Performance)) checks you make to amuse Humanoid onlookers while the cloak is billowing.

*Source: Stranger Things: Welcome to the Hellfire Club*
