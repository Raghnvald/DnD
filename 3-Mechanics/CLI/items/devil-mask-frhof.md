# Devil Mask
*Adventuring gear*  
![](3-Mechanics/CLI/items/img/devil-mask.webp#right)

- **Cost**: 25 gp

*Grant your sins plausible deniability by wearing one of these diabolical masks inspired by fiends from the Nine Hells. Also suitable for patriar galas in the city of Baldur's Gate.*

While you are wearing a Devil Mask, other creatures have <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Disadvantage</span> on Intelligence (Investigation) and Wisdom (Insight) checks made to discern your true identity or intentions.

*Source: Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn p. 132*
