# Winter's Dark Bite
*Weapon (greatsword), uncommon*  


- **Damage**: 2d6 slashing
- **Properties**: Heavy, Two-Handed
- **Weight**: 6.0 lbs.

You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

This greatsword is made of an unknown black metal. In most cases, it works as a +1 greatsword. But when used against a [thessalhydra](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/thessalhydra-hftt.md), it works as a +3 greatsword. While in the Upside Down, it works as a +4 greatsword.

*Source: Hunt for the Thessalhydra p. 4*
