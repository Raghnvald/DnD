# Concussion Grenade
*Explosive*  
![Left (Top to Bottom): Needler Pistol, Paralysis Pistol, Bandolier of Grenades. Middle: Antigravity Belt. Right (Top to Bottom): Laser Pistol, Robot Controller, Laser Rifle](3-Mechanics/CLI/items/img/technology.webp#right)


This grenade explodes in a concussive blast that fills a 20-foot-radius sphere. Each creature in that area must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking (`6d6`) force damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

*Source: Quests from the Infinite Staircase p. 192*
