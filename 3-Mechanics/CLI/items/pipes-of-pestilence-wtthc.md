# Pipes of Pestilence
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*  
![](3-Mechanics/CLI/items/img/pipes-of-pestilence.webp#right)


If you play these pipes as a [Magic](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/actions.md#Magic) action, you summon one [Swarm of Corrupted Rats](3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/swarm-of-corrupted-rats-wtthc.md) in an unoccupied space within 60 feet of yourself. The swarm is [Friendly](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/friendly-attitude-xphb.md) to you and your allies for as long as you continue to play the pipes each round as a [Magic](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/actions.md#Magic) action. While [Friendly](3-Mechanics/CLI/rules/variant-rules/friendly-attitude-xphb.md), the swarm is under your control, obeys your commands, and takes no action other than to defend itself if you issue it no commands. Once your control ends, the swarm vanishes, and the pipes can't be used again until the next dawn.

*Source: Stranger Things: Welcome to the Hellfire Club*
