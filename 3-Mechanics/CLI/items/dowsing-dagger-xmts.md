# Dowsing Dagger



A curved jade blade fashioned by the River Heralds, this [+1 dagger](3-Mechanics/CLI/items/1-weapon-xdmg.md) can detect the presence of fresh water within 200 feet of the wielder.

*Source: X Marks the Spot p. 19*
