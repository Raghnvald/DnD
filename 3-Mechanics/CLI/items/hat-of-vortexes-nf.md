# Hat of Vortexes
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*  



This hat has 3 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. You can take a Magic action and expend 1 charge while holding the hat to release a magical vortex from it. The vortex fills a 10-foot <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Cube</span> originating from you and lasts for 1 hour. While the vortex is present, its area is <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Difficult Terrain</span>.

You decide a vortex's visual details when you create it. For instance, the vortex might be multicolored or glittery.

*Source: Netheril's Fall*
