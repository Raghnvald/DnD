# Primal Amulet



This beaded jade necklace bears an ancient symbol of the River Heralds. When worn, the primal amulet allows its wearer to cast [speak with animals](3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/speak-with-animals-xphb.md), [locate object](3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/locate-object-xphb.md), and [pass without trace](3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/pass-without-trace-xphb.md). Once the amulet has been used to cast a spell, it can't be used to cast that spell again until the next dawn.

*Source: X Marks the Spot p. 19*
