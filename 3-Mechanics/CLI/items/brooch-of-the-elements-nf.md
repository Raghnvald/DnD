# Brooch of the Elements
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*  



The central jewel of this butterfly-shaped brooch swirls with multicolored iridescence.

The brooch has 3 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. As a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> when a creature you can see within 60 feet of you hits a target with an attack roll and deals damage, you can expend 1 charge and change one damage type the attack deals to one of the following damage types: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison, or Thunder.

*Source: Netheril's Fall*
