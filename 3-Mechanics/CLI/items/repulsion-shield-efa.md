# Repulsion Shield
*Armor (shield), uncommon*  


- **Armor Class**: 2
- **Weight**: 6.0 lbs.

You gain a +1 bonus to <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Armor Class</span> while wielding this shield.

The shield has 4 charges. While holding it, when a Large or smaller creature within 5 feet of you hits you with a melee attack roll, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> to expend 1 of the shield's charges and push the attacker up to 15 feet straight away. The shield regains `1d4` expended charges daily at dawn.

*Source: Eberron: Forge of the Artificer p. 112*
